QUESTIONS WE GET

Straight answers.

I'm in WA / QLD / SA — do you actually work in my state? +
Yes. We service NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA and TAS. Drop your postcode in the application — that's how we route the right jobs to you. If we're still light on builders in your patch, we'll tell you straight rather than string you along.
What kind of work is on the book? +
Commercial fitouts, retail rollouts, hospitality, healthcare, education. Repeat-builder relationships, not one-off resi flips. The book is built for crews that want to be booked through the year — not chasing the next quote every Friday.
What about payment terms? I've been burned before. +
Weekly invoicing cycle. Invoice the job, get paid that week. No 30/60/90, no chasing accounts, no "we'll get to it." It's the single biggest thing the network is built around — because we know it's the single biggest reason good subbies disappear.
Do I need to be available full-time? +
No. There's a casual / overflow option in the application. If you've got capacity but only sometimes — busy season, weekends, the odd week — that's still useful to us. We'll route the right kind of work your way.
Will you call my reference without my say-so? +
Never. We talk to you first. References get called only after we've had a conversation with you and you've okayed it. We've been the subbie getting called behind our back — we don't do it to ours.
What if I don't have $10M PL cover yet? +
It's a hard line. Every commercial job we run requires $10M minimum, and that's the floor. Sort the cover first, then come back — the application picks up where you left off, no extra forms.
Is this labour-hire? Am I working for Vaquero? +
No. You run your own crew, your own ABN, your own gear. Vaquero is the commercial flooring contractor on the job — we hand you the work and pay you for it. You're a subcontractor in the network, not on the payroll. That's the relationship, and it's what keeps it clean for both sides.
What if I'm not a fit? +
You'll hear from us either way. If the book is full for your specialty or your patch, we'll tell you — and we'll keep your details on file. The network expands as the build pipeline does. "Not now" doesn't mean "not ever."
